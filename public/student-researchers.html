Submit Your Output

Use the research submission form to submit capstone projects, student theses, or other research outputs. Be sure to choose the correct research type and attach all required documents.

  • Title, authors, program, adviser, and research category
  • Choose Capstone or Student Thesis for research type
  • Upload manuscript, abstract, approval forms, similarity report, adviser endorsement, and panel sheet
  • Optional supporting files: ethics certificate, publication proof, and additional documents

Required Documents for Student Submissions

Manuscript

Complete research paper or thesis document.

Abstract

Executive summary of your research.

Approval Form

Evidence that the research has institutional approval.

Similarity Report

Academic integrity check report.

Adviser Endorsement

Written approval from your research adviser.

Panel Approval Sheet

Confirmation of capstone/project panel approval.

Submission Tips

  • Log in as a student researcher before submitting.
  • Keep document filenames clear and descriptive.
  • Review all required files before you click submit.
  • If revisions are requested, upload a revised manuscript through the submission detail page.
  • Contact your adviser or CPRI staff for help with ethics clearance.
