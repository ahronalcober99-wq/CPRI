About CPRI

Vision, mission, goals, organizational structure, and functions of the Center.

Vision

Colegio de San Gabriel Arcangel, Inc., through its research arm, the Center for Publication, Research, and Innovation (CPRI), is envisioned to achieve a reputation as an excellent multidisciplinary research center that pursues research breakthroughs toward community development and attainment of institutional ideals and goals.

Mission

The Center for Publication, Research, and Innovation (CPRI) commits itself to the realization and sustainability of a strong culture of research, promoting high-quality and innovative research carried out by high ethical standards and providing opportunities to conduct, present, and publish researches locally, nationally, and internationally.

Goals and Objectives

  • Conduct rigorous, policy-relevant research across priority development areas.
  • Translate research evidence into actionable policy recommendations.
  • Strengthen research capability of faculty, students, and partner institutions.
  • Promote open, ethical, and collaborative research practices.
  • Engage communities and decision-makers in co-creating solutions.

Organizational Structure

CPRI reports to the College President through the Vice President for Research, Publication, and External Affairs, and is led by the Center Director. Revised 2023.

Functions of the Center

Research

Design and implement studies addressing pressing policy and development issues.

Policy Advisory

Provide evidence-based recommendations to government and institutions.

Capability Building

Deliver trainings and mentorship to grow the research workforce.

Knowledge Dissemination

Publish briefs, papers, and maintain an open research repository.

Partnerships

Build linkages with universities, agencies, and communities.

Ethics Oversight

Ensure responsible conduct and integrity in all research activities.

Research Policies and Guidelines

  • All studies follow established research ethics and data privacy standards.
  • Open access is the default for CPRI-funded publications.
  • Authorship and acknowledgement guidelines ensure fair credit.
  • Research involving human participants requires ethics clearance.
  • Data management plans are required at project inception.
