Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2026

1. Information We Collect

CPRI collects the following types of information when you use our website, register for an account, submit research, or contact us:

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, email address, username, institutional affiliation, role/title
  • Account Credentials: Username and password (stored as a secure hash)
  • Profile Information: Department, contact number, research interests, profile photo
  • Research Data: Manuscripts, abstracts, ethics applications, publication metadata
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, search queries, download activity
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, session cookies

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the CPRI research platform
  • To process research submissions, ethics reviews, and publication workflows
  • To communicate with you about your account, submissions, and updates
  • To generate aggregated analytics and research impact reports
  • To improve our services and user experience
  • To comply with legal obligations and institutional policies

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers with industry-standard encryption. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

4. Cookies

We use essential session cookies to maintain your login state and authentication. Optional analytics cookies may be used to improve our service. You can manage cookie preferences through our cookie consent banner.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share aggregated, anonymized data for research reporting purposes. Your research submissions may be published in the CPRI repository with your consent and appropriate attribution.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data held by CPRI
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing where applicable
  • Export your data in a portable format

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Research submissions and publications are retained in accordance with institutional retention policies and may be archived for academic record purposes.

8. Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — for typography
  • Bootstrap Icons / Font Awesome — for iconography
  • Chart.js — for data visualization
  • CDN providers (jsDelivr, cdnjs, unpkg) — for library distribution
